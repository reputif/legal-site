Reputif Privacy Policy

This policy describes how the Reputif iOS app (bundle identifier com.reputif.atelier) handles information on your iPhone. Reputif is a local greeting-card studio. It does not operate an account system and it does not upload your cards.

Information that stays on your device

When you build a card, Reputif keeps these items in the app's private storage on this device:

  • Photos you capture with the camera or import from your photo library, plus the finished card image.
  • The greeting message you type, and the spoken words captured as audio and turned into text when you use Voice Input.
  • The foil signature, frame, layout, and draft state of each card.
  • The Folio balance and purchase records needed to show what you have already bought on this installation.

These photos, voice recordings, and device records are not copied to a Reputif server. Uninstalling the app, or using Clear All Data in Settings, removes them from this installation.

Permissions you agree to

Reputif asks for a system permission only when you tap the related control. You can decline. The feature then stays unavailable and the rest of the app continues.

  • Camera: used only to capture a photo that becomes the picture on a card, after you agree.
  • Photo library: used to read a photo you pick, and to save a finished card back to Photos when you ask.
  • Microphone and speech recognition: used to hear a spoken message and place the words into the caption field, after you agree. You can stop listening at any time and edit the text.
  • Notifications: used only if you tap Allow Reminders, so the system can ask whether this device may show a local reminder.

Purchases

Folio packs are consumable in-app purchases sold by Apple. Payment, receipts, and the Apple account are handled by Apple under Apple's own terms. Reputif stores the resulting Folio count on this device so a finished render can spend one Folio. Reputif does not receive your card number.

Third-party sharing

Reputif does not send your photos, audio, voice, messages, or device data to a third-party service. There is no third-party analytics or advertising network in the app. The only external party involved in a purchase is Apple, acting as the store that processes the payment you start.

Children

Reputif is a general stationery tool. It does not knowingly collect personal information from children.
